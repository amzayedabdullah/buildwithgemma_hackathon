# 🎥 Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই) — 3-Minute YouTube Video Presentation Script
**Competition**: Build With Gemma @ Bangladesh Hackathon 2026  
**Track**: Track 6 — Native Audio & Voice Track  
**Target Video Duration**: 2 minutes 50 seconds (Under 5-minute strict limit)  

---

## 🎬 Shot-by-Shot Video Script

### **00:00 - 00:35 | Section 1: The Problem (Hook & Bangladesh Context)**
- **Visual**: Footage / B-roll of a busy *Mudi Dokan* (grocery store) in Dhaka/Chittagong. Shopkeeper frantically writing on a worn-out paper ledger while customers shout orders.
- **Voiceover (Plain Language)**:
  > *"Meet Uncle Rahim. He runs a small neighborhood Mudi Dokan in Bangladesh. Every day, dozens of customers buy rice, oil, and spices on credit — what we call 'Baki'. Like 15 million shopkeepers across Bangladesh, Rahim records over $50 Billion in micro-credit using paper Khatas. But paper gets lost, calculation errors happen, and busy shopkeepers don't have time to manually type into complicated mobile apps during rush hours. Even worse, regional dialects make standard voice typing useless. Until now."*

---

### **00:35 - 01:40 | Section 2: Product Demo & Gemma Integration**
- **Visual**: Screen capture of **Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই)** web application running on desktop and mobile.
- **Action**: Demonstrator clicks the microphone icon and speaks in Bangla:
  > *"রহিম সাহেব ২০০ টাকার সয়াবিন তেল বাকিতে নিলেন আর ৫০ টাকা ক্যাশ দিলেন।"*
- **Visual**: Real-time visual waveform animating, followed by Gemma parsing the speech in under 1 second into structured cards:
  - Customer: Rahim (রহিম সাহেব)
  - Total Baki Balance updated live.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"This is Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই) — powered by Google Gemma. Rahim simply taps one button and speaks naturally in conversational Bangla or regional dialects. Gemma's native language understanding instantly extracts the customer's name, items purchased, cash paid, and remaining debt into a clean financial ledger. No manual typing, no complex dropdowns."*

---

### **01:40 - 02:20 | Section 3: Value Beyond Chatbot & Debt Collection**
- **Visual**: Demonstrator shows the **Gemma-Powered Relationship-Preserving Payment Reminder Generator**.
- **Action**: Demonstrator clicks "Generate WhatsApp Reminder" for a 14-day overdue Baki customer.
- **Visual**: Gemma outputs a polite, respectful Bangla WhatsApp text message:
  > *"আসসালামু আলাইকুম করিম ভাই, আশা করি ভালো আছেন। Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই) থেকে আপনার ২০০ টাকার বাকি হিসাবটি আপডেট করা হয়েছে। সময় সুযোগ মতো এসে বাকি টাকা পরিশোধ করলে উপকৃত হবো। ধন্যবাদ!"*
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই) goes far beyond a generic chatbot. It automates financial ledger bookkeeping, calculates real-time credit risks, and acts as a social mediator. Using Gemma, it generates culturally appropriate, respectful Bangla payment reminders for WhatsApp or SMS — allowing shopkeepers to recover overdue debts without ruining community relationships."*

---

### **02:20 - 02:50 | Section 4: Architecture & Closing**
- **Visual**: Architecture Diagram highlighting Gemma API / Google AI Studio integration, Next.js UI, and offline LocalStorage caching.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Built with Next.js and integrated with Google Gemma open models, Hisab.AI (হিসাব.এআই) can run on modest hardware and low-connectivity environments across Bangladesh. By giving 15 million shopkeepers a voice-first AI assistant, Gemma is transforming informal micro-finance into an accessible digital economy. Thank you!"*
